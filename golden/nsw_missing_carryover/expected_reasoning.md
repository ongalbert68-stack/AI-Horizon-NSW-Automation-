# Expected reasoning -- nsw_missing_carryover

Plain-language version of `expected.json`. Where the two disagree,
`expected.json` is what a script grades against.

## What this image is

`img/missing_carryover.png` was generated by
`vision/synth/defects.py::missing_carryover`, which walks the dots in
dispense order and, one time in five, skips a dot and carries its material
onto the next one. Its docstring says outright that this is the mechanism
matc82 sec 3.5 documents.

So unlike the paired inconsistent-size case, **the correct cause is known
here**. That is the point of having both: one case where the carryover
answer is right, one where it is wrong, so an engine cannot pass by always
preferring it.

## What the measurements show

From `cv_output.json` and `signals.json`:

- Three positions have no deposit at all: dots 0, 6 and 9 in dispense order.
- Three deposits are oversized, at 3.41x to 4.01x the recipe's nominal area:
  dots 1, 7 and 10.
- **Every miss is followed immediately by an oversized deposit**, 3 of 3.
  With 3 oversized deposits among 14 possible following positions, chance
  alone produces that about 1% of the time.
- The other nine deposits measure 0.85x to 1.01x nominal, so the rest of the
  array is in spec.
- Mean circularity is 0.889, so the oversized deposits are round. This is
  not smearing or tailing.

### A fixture limitation to know about

The generator adds the carried **radius** to the next dot, so a carry
measures 3.4x to 4.0x nominal **area**. A carry that conserved volume would
be nearer 2x. Real deposits would not be this large, so an engine must not
be expected to match deficit against surplus, and must not treat the
surplus being bigger than the missing volume as an argument against
carryover.

## What a correct analysis looks like

Name the defect as missing deposits each followed by an oversized deposit,
then rank **volume too small to wet the pad (big dot / small dot)** first.
The measurements match matc82's hallmark exactly, on every miss.

Second place can reasonably go to:

- **Pressure-time instability**, which matc82 gives as an eventual cause of
  missed dots (M10, M18), and which explains why a shot's volume could be
  marginal in the first place. It does not explain the pairing.
- **Air in the fluid path**, which the brief has causing volume to vary. No
  source has air adding the missing volume to the next dot.

**Nozzle blockage must not come first.** A restriction starves shots; no
source says it deposits extra material on the following shot, so it leaves
the three oversized deposits unexplained.

The fix comes from the manual (M12, M16, M20): increase the dispense time,
or keep the volume down with a smaller ID foot contact needle. No source
gives a percentage, so neither should an answer.

## How the existing system does today

Measured by running `web/engines/deterministic.ts` directly, not from memory.

| Input | Result |
|---|---|
| Complaint only | nozzle blockage 48%, air entrapment 30% |
| Complaint + `vision/main.py` signals | air entrapment 51%, nozzle blockage 48%, needle height 28% |

**Fails.** The pack has no carryover cause at all, so the correct answer
cannot be ranked first, or even named. Its recommended first actions are to
inspect the syringe for voids and to pull the needle, neither of which
addresses a marginal dispense volume.

Two related gaps:

- `NSW_FOLLOWUPS.missing` asks whether the shot after a missing dot recovers
  to normal size, and offers only "the next shot is normal" or "several
  shots stay undersized". The observation in this image, that the next shot
  is oversized, cannot be entered.
- The needle-height rule again fires on `spread_index` (3.792 here) and
  claims deposits are spread, though circularity is 0.889.

Fixing this needs a carryover cause in `web/packs/nsw/index.ts`, a rule that
fires on the dispense-order signal rather than on size spread alone, and a
third option on that follow-up question.

## Known issues this case documents

1. `vision/main.py` reports `position_deviation_px` 7.47 here against 0.55
   for the paired case, because three gaps in the array distort the lattice
   fit it measures against. The position score drops for a reason that has
   nothing to do with placement.
2. `vision/main.py` returns no per-dot tags and no dispense order, so
   `small_then_big_count` -- the signal this whole case turns on -- cannot be
   computed from what the app currently receives. `vision/detect/` provides
   both.
